# Student Affairs gaps: sanction history, manual incidents, concern investigations, investigation scheduling

Date: 2026-11-19. Status: awaiting approval.

## Intent and why

Five gaps in the Student Affairs (Discipline Officer, "DO") workflow:

1. A student's **sanction history** can't be seen in one place, and students and
   parents can't see it at all.
2. The DO can't **record an incident or violation by hand** for a specific
   student; incidents only arrive through reports or scans.
3. A **concern** a student files can only be endorsed to Guidance. Once Guidance
   returns it, the concern shows only the text "Endorsed": no control and none of
   the meeting's findings. There is also no way to send a concern into the
   investigation due process.
4. **Investigation meetings** are scheduled differently from endorsement
   meetings. The DO wants the same flow (ask the parent's availability, then
   approve a time), with the DO as the only approver because an investigation
   that was not endorsed does not involve the guidance counselor.

## Goals

- Staff see a student's full sanction history; the student and the parent see
  their own in their portals.
- The DO can manually report an incident for any student, with an audit trail.
- A concern can be sent to investigation. The DO names the student it is about
  at that moment.
- An endorsed concern that comes back shows Guidance's findings and outcome when
  expanded, and keeps its status picker.
- A concern can always be updated or closed with the status picker.
- Investigation meetings use the endorsement flow, approved by the DO alone.

## Non-goals

- Production-grade security for credential parents (see Constraints).
- Changing how endorsement scheduling behaves today.
- Putting a "who is this about" field back on the student's concern form.
- Showing the reporter the accused student's findings or discipline.

## Anti-goals

- Breaking or regressing the endorsement flow (availability, Proven / Not Proven,
  sanctions, tests).
- Letting a student or parent see internal sanction data (override reasons, who
  decided, handbook basis).
- A concern getting stuck with no way for the DO to close it.

## Constraints

- **Credential parents have no Supabase Auth user.** They and the demo accounts
  run on the `anon` role, so `auth.uid()` is null. Any parent-facing read or
  write needs an `anon` path, as `supabase/add_parent_credential_access.sql`
  does (anon select policies plus an optional `p_parent_id`). This is test-grade:
  anyone with the anon key who knows a parent's id can act as that parent.
  Students may be in the same situation; confirm per portal.
- **Investigations hang off violations.** `guidance_investigations.violation_id`
  is required and unique, and `escalate_to_guidance(violation_id)` starts one.
  A concern is not a violation, so sending a concern to investigation first
  needs an incident record.
- Migrations are idempotent SQL files run by hand in the Supabase SQL editor.
  New SQL tests follow the existing `supabase/test_*.sql` style, run as an
  impersonated Admin.
- Windows files use CRLF; SQL generated by scripts must normalise line endings.

## Approaches considered

- **A. Extend what exists (chosen).** Availability rounds and meetings can
  belong to either an endorsement or an investigation. Investigation meetings
  reuse the same request / offer / approve functions with a DO-only approval
  branch. Concerns gain a link to the investigation they started. One flow, no
  drift, at the cost of editing shipped code carefully.
- **B. Parallel investigation-only tables and functions.** Isolated from the
  endorsement flow but duplicates it, so the two diverge and every fix is
  needed twice. Rejected.
- **C. Mock the screens first, wire the database later.** Early visuals but
  nothing real until a second pass; a poor fit for database-heavy work.
  Rejected.

## Decisions

### 1. Sanction history

- A read view over the existing `sanctions` table (one row per violation).
- **Staff:** a per-student history in the DO's violation history area and
  reachable from the student's record. Full detail.
- **Student and parent portals:** a card on each home page, hidden when there
  are no sanctions. Shows only: sanction type, short description, effective and
  end dates, whether it affects enrollment. Never `override_reason`,
  `decided_by` or the handbook basis.
- Parent access uses an `anon`-callable read that checks the parent's link to
  the student (`parent_student_links`), like the availability fix.

### 2. Student Affairs manual incident report

- A panel in the DO dashboard. Fields: student (search), offense (handbook
  offense or custom), description, date and place of the incident.
- Creates a violation record marked as reported by Student Affairs, writes an
  audit-log entry, and appears in the normal violations list and history.
- **Recorded quietly (decided by you):** saving the incident sends no message to
  the parent or student. The DO decides separately whether to notify the parent,
  with an explicit "Notify parent" choice (a switch on the form, off by default,
  and the same action available later from the incident). Nothing is sent
  unless the DO chooses it.
- It is the same form that "Send to investigation" opens, pre-filled (see 3).
  Starting an investigation likewise does not notify anyone by itself.

### 3. Concerns

- **Send to investigation** (new action beside "Endorse to Guidance"): opens
  the incident form pre-filled from the concern. The DO picks the student it is
  about (the form never asks the reporter). On save: the violation is created
  with a link to the concern, the investigation starts, and the concern moves
  to `In_Progress` and shows "Under investigation".
- **Status picker always available**, including on an endorsed concern. This
  replaces the earlier "only an investigation can close it" idea; the latest
  instruction is the rule.
- **Fix the returned-endorsement gap:** when an endorsed concern is expanded it
  shows Guidance's findings, recommendation and outcome from the endorsement and
  its meeting, and keeps the picker instead of the bare "Endorsed" text.
- The reporter keeps seeing only the status and the handler note, as today.

### 4. Investigation scheduling

- `meeting_availability_rounds` may belong to an investigation instead of an
  endorsement; exactly one of the two links is set.
- The investigation view gets the same controls as endorsements: request the
  parent's availability, see offered times, approve one or decline all.
- **Approval is by the DO only.** Notifications for investigation rounds go to
  the DO and parent, not to the guidance counselor.
- Meetings created from an approved time are investigation meetings that the
  parent confirms or declines in the Parent Portal like any invitation.
- The parent side (availability card, meeting card, anon access) is unchanged.

### 5. Completed meetings land in the intervention log

- "Intervention logs" means the Guidance Counselor's confidential Student
  Archive (`counseling_archive_logs`, shown in the counselor's Student Archive
  tab).
- When a **guidance-owned meeting** (endorsement or self-referral meeting) is
  marked **Completed** through `set_meeting_outcome`, the database adds one log
  entry for that student automatically. No counselor action is needed.
- Entry: log type `parent_conference` when the parent is invited (parent or
  both), `counseling` when only the student is; occurred on the meeting date;
  title says what the meeting was; notes carry who was invited, date and time,
  location, the invitee's response note, and the findings or recommendation if
  Guidance recorded them.
- The entry links back to its meeting (a `meeting_id` column, unique) so
  completing or re-saving never creates a duplicate.
- It is written by the database function, not by the counselor's session, so it
  works whoever marks the meeting Completed (counselor, DO or Admin) and stays
  readable only to the Guidance Counselor and Admin, as the archive already is.
- Missed and Cancelled meetings are not logged.
- Investigation meetings (DO-only, no counselor) are not logged here, because
  the archive is the counselor's record.

## Delivery order

Five separate increments, each tested and merged on its own:

1. Sanction history, including the student and parent portals.
2. Manual incident report.
3. Concerns: send to investigation, and the returned-endorsement fix.
4. Investigation scheduling.
5. Completed meetings auto-logged to the intervention log. This is small and
   independent, so it can go earlier if you want it sooner.

(Increment 3 needs increment 2's incident form.)

## Calls I made (change any of these)

- Closing a concern while its investigation is still open is allowed; the
  investigation continues on its own.
- Student and parent sanction cards use the field list above and nothing more.
- Investigation rounds notify the DO and parent only.
- Only Completed meetings are logged; Missed and Cancelled are not.
- Investigation meetings are not written to the counselor's archive.
- Meetings completed before this ships are not backfilled.

## Left to the builder

- Exact SQL names, indexes and the check that exactly one of
  `escalation_report_id` / `investigation_id` is set on a round.
- Whether the student portal needs its own `anon` read path (depends on how the
  student logs in; verify rather than assume).
- Where the sanction history entry point sits in the DO and Guidance screens.
- Test plan: extend the existing `supabase/test_*.sql` scripts and the Flutter
  widget tests, and re-run the endorsement and availability suites after
  increment 4.
